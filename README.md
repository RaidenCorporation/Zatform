**Zatform** is an online platformer game where you fight other players and bosses and collect coins to buy new skins and weapons.

# 🖥️ Platform Availability
Zatform is available as an Android APK, as there might be an upcoming Windows release in the future.

---

# 📩 How To Download
1. Look for the latest APK from the latest release on the [Releases](https://github.com/RaidenCorporation/Zatform/releases) tab.
2. Scroll down on the latest release to see “Assets”.
3. In the Assets section, look for **app-release.apk** and click it.
4. Wait for it to install.
5. Go to your regular Files app.
6. Look for app-release.apk.
7. Once you found the file, press the 3 dots ⋮ or hold the file and press "Install".
8. It will be installed to your device. Enjoy!

---

# ⚙️ Core Features
**Online:** Zatform is an online game, meaning you can engage with other players and fight with them.
**Bosses:** Pick up a stone using the pickup button next to a stone and click the attack button to break a cage to free a boss that follows and attacks you.
**Multiplayer Co-op:** Click your profile in the top left corner and scroll down to find the Multiplayer section. The game will create a unique room code, which you can then share to your friends and they will enter the code and you can play with each other. Note that you cannot attack your friends in co-op mode, and they cannot attack you.
**Weapons & Cosmetics Shop:** A weapons shop to buy better, stronger weapons and a cosmetics shop to buy new colours and hats.
